# 📱 Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment methods, and business growth using dynamic dashboards and advanced DAX calculations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of mobile phone sales by transforming raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

It enables decision-makers to monitor sales performance, identify top-performing products, analyze customer preferences, evaluate payment methods, and measure business growth through interactive reports.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Track sales trends over time.
- Compare city-wise sales performance.
- Analyze customer ratings.
- Evaluate payment method preferences.
- Identify top-selling mobile models.
- Measure business growth using Time Intelligence.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 📄 Page 1 – Sales Performance Dashboard

Provides an executive overview of business performance.

### Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Transactions
- Average Selling Price

### Visualizations

- City-wise Sales Analysis
- Monthly Quantity Trend
- Customer Ratings Distribution
- Payment Method Analysis
- Top Selling Mobile Models
- Geographic Sales Distribution (Map)

---

## 📄 Page 2 – MTD, QTD & YTD Analysis

Designed to monitor business performance across different time periods.

### Key Analysis

- Month-to-Date (MTD)
- Quarter-to-Date (QTD)
- Year-to-Date (YTD)
- Monthly Growth Trend
- Quarterly Sales Analysis

Business users can quickly evaluate current business performance against targets.

---

## 📄 Page 3 – Same Period Last Year Analysis

Compares current performance with historical data.

### Includes

- Same Period Last Year (SPLY)
- Year-over-Year Growth
- Monthly Comparison
- Quarterly Comparison
- Daily Sales Trend

This page helps identify business growth patterns and seasonal performance.
